# Cover

## Roles
- Student: views courses, enrolls, checks progress, and submits requests.
- Professor: manages classes, attendance, assignments, materials, quizzes, and grades.
- Teaching Assistant: supports teaching, grading, and student help.
- Academic Advisor: guides students and reviews academic plans.
- Registrar: manages enrollment, records, schedules, and student data.
- Admissions Officer: handles applications, interviews, and offers.
- Finance Staff: manages fees, balances, payments, and finance approvals.
- IT Admin: manages users, access, system settings, and support.
- Dean: oversees academic operations and reports.
- HOD: manages department courses, staff, and academic work.
- Librarian: supports library access and records.
- Super Admin: controls everything in the system.
- Student Affairs: handles student support and welfare.
- HR Staff: manages staff records and onboarding.
- Security Officer: handles security, access, and visitor control.
- Facilities Manager: handles maintenance and campus services.
- Research Officer: manages research work and approvals.

## Features
- Role-based dashboards.
- Student and professor management.
- Enrollment and class management.
- Admissions and applications.
- Finance tracking and approvals.
- News and feedback.
- Audit logs.
- User management.
- Settings and requests.

## Important Features
- Fast role-based access.
- Clear navigation for each user type.
- Search and quick record lookup.
- Student, professor, and finance workflows.
- Admin control and system monitoring.
